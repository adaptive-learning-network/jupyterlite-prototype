It does **not** reproduce the original interactive forms, flashcards, or external media offline. The archived Unit 1 lesson has six Google Drive image endpoints that do not render reliably in JupyterLite; the import keeps links to those illustrations. Source links and a revision hash appear at the end of each page.

That outline is context, not a claim that the two decks are identical.
